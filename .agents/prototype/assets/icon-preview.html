Two window panes, slightly offset. The mark refers to Android apps opening in separate Mac windows. Solid shapes keep the silhouette readable at small sizes; a single title-bar stroke makes the foreground pane recognizable.

Original SVG artwork. The same master is used in the sidebar, desktop dock, menu bar and browser favicon. Native release assets will need platform-specific optical sizing and export.
